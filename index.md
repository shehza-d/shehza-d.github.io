Hello! I am a software developer and educator based in Karachi. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and education: I build practical web products, explain complex technical ideas, and create learning experiences that help people progress.

I work primarily across the TypeScript ecosystem, from responsive React and Next.js interfaces to Node.js services. I currently teach at **Saylani Mass IT Training** and **Bahria College**, and I am expanding my work toward secure software engineering and cybersecurity.

I enjoy building in public, maintaining learning resources, and working with people who care about useful software and accessible technical education.
